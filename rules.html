Clan Rules

Follow these to survive in the Legions

1

Be respectful to all clan members.

2

Stay active. Players offline for more than 7 days without informing a leader will be kicked.

3

Donate troops fairly. Everyone should contribute and help fellow clan members.

4

Request and donate the correct troops whenever possible.

5

Be ready to participate in Clan Wars and Clan War Leagues.

6

If you are included in a war, you must use all your attacks.

7

Always attack your assigned target unless a leader says otherwise.

8

Opt out of wars if you know you won't be available.

9

Support the clan by participating in Clan Games and other clan events.

10

Inactive, non-donating, or repeatedly missing war attacks may result in demotion or removal.

11

Follow leader and co-leader instructions during wars.

12

Have fun, help your clanmates, and work together to grow stronger.
